Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical laptop repair take?

Most software issues, screen replacements and battery jobs are completed same-day. Chip-level motherboard work usually takes 1–3 working days.

Do you offer pickup and delivery?

Yes — free pickup & drop is available within Sodepur, Sukchar, Khardah, Agarpara, Panihati and nearby areas in North Kolkata.

Do you use original spare parts?

Absolutely. We use genuine or OEM-grade parts only and offer a warranty on every component replaced.

What brands do you service?

Dell, HP, Lenovo, MSI, Acer, Asus, MacBook (Apple), Samsung, Sony, Toshiba, LG, Microsoft Surface and more.
